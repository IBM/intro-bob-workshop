# Lab 2 Comparison: Standard vs. EDT Edition

## Overview

This document compares the two versions of Lab 2 to help you choose which is best for your high school workshop.

---

## Quick Comparison Table

| Aspect | Standard Lab 2 | EDT Edition Lab 2 |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Duration** | 90 minutes | 90 minutes |
| **Prerequisites** | Basic computer literacy | EDT flower vase activity completed |
| **Primary Focus** | General agentic AI capabilities | Design thinking → AI implementation |
| **First Exercise** | Generic Bob introduction | EDT bridge activity |
| **Best For** | Standalone AI workshop | Follow-up to EDT session |
| **Key Theme** | "What can Bob do?" | "How does AI complete the design cycle?" |
| **Student Preparation** | None required | Brings EDT sketches/ideas |

---

## Detailed Comparison

### Part 1: Introduction (First 10-15 minutes)

**Standard Lab 2:**
- **Exercise 1.1:** First Conversation (5 min)
  - Students ask Bob to introduce himself
  - Learn about agentic AI vs. chatbots
  - Understand Bob's tools and capabilities

- **Exercise 1.2:** Exploring Your Project (5 min)
  - Bob explores project directory
  - Students see file operations in action
  - Introduction to Bob's analytical abilities

**EDT Edition:**
- **Part 1:** Getting to Know Bob (5 min)
  - Quick instructor-led demo
  - Brief explanation of agentic AI
  - Faster introduction to get to EDT bridge

- **Part 2:** From Design Thinking to Reality (20 min)
  - Reflect on EDT flower vase activity
  - Choose a reframed solution
  - Watch Bob implement design thinking concepts
  - See ideas become working prototypes

**Key Difference:** EDT edition immediately connects to previous day's work, creating continuity and showing the complete design-to-implementation cycle.

---

### Part 2/3: Creative Work (20-25 minutes)

**Standard Lab 2:**
- **Exercise 2.1:** Create an Interactive Game (25 min)
  - Build a browser-based game
  - Focus on fun and engagement
  - Learn about game logic and interactivity

**EDT Edition:**
- **Part 3:** Creative Projects (20 min)
  - Apply design thinking to new problems
  - Practice problem reframing
  - Build experience-focused solutions
  - Shorter to accommodate EDT bridge

**Key Difference:** EDT edition emphasizes design thinking methodology throughout, while standard focuses more on technical capabilities.

---

### Part 3/4: Code Analysis (15-20 minutes)

**Standard Lab 2:**
- **Exercise 3.1:** Code Detective & Improvement (20 min)
  - Read and analyze existing code
  - Identify improvements
  - Implement enhancements
  - Learn about iteration

**EDT Edition:**
- **Part 4:** Code Analysis & Iteration (15 min)
  - Similar content but condensed
  - Emphasizes iteration as part of design thinking
  - Can be made optional if time is short

**Key Difference:** Similar content, but EDT edition is more flexible with timing.

---

### Part 4/5: Real-World Applications (20-25 minutes)

**Standard Lab 2:**
- **Exercise 4.1:** Build Your Own Project (25 min)
  - Choose from project ideas
  - Build something unique
  - Practice iteration
  - Apply all learned skills

**EDT Edition:**
- **Part 5:** Your Design Challenge (20 min)
  - Structured around design thinking framework
  - Empathize → Define → Ideate → Prototype → Test
  - Solve real problems with user-centered approach
  - Explicit connection to EDT principles

**Key Difference:** EDT edition provides explicit design thinking framework, while standard is more open-ended.

---

### Part 5/6: Wrap-Up (10 minutes)

**Standard Lab 2:**
- Group sharing
- Key takeaways about agentic AI
- Reflection on AI capabilities
- Discussion of limitations and ethics

**EDT Edition:**
- Group sharing with design thinking lens
- Key takeaways about human-AI partnership
- Reflection on complete design cycle
- Discussion of EDT + AI integration
- Ethics in AI-assisted design

**Key Difference:** EDT edition explicitly connects back to design thinking principles and the human-AI partnership.

## When to Use Each Version

### Use Standard Lab 2 When:

✅ **Standalone Workshop**
- Students haven't done EDT activities
- This is their first exposure to design thinking
- Focus is purely on AI capabilities
- No prior context needed

✅ **Technical Focus**
- Goal is to teach AI and coding concepts
- Less emphasis on design methodology
- More time for technical exploration
- Students are interested in how AI works

✅ **Flexible Scheduling**
- Workshop is not tied to other activities
- Can be scheduled anytime
- No coordination with other instructors needed

✅ **Broader Audience**
- Mixed group with different backgrounds
- Some students may have done EDT, others haven't
- Need a universal starting point

---

### Use EDT Edition When:

✅ **Following EDT Session**
- Students completed flower vase activity previous day
- Want to build on design thinking concepts
- Create continuity between sessions
- Show complete design cycle

✅ **Design Thinking Focus**
- Goal is to teach design methodology + AI
- Emphasize human-centered design
- Show how AI fits into design process
- Connect to IBM's EDT framework

✅ **Coordinated Program**
- Part of multi-day workshop or camp
- Coordinating with other instructors
- Building progressive learning experience
- Want strong narrative arc

✅ **Career Exploration**
- Showing students design careers
- Demonstrating modern design tools
- Emphasizing human creativity + AI capability
- Preparing for design-related fields

## Instructor Considerations

### Standard Lab 2 Requires:

**Preparation:**
- Test Bob functionality
- Prepare example projects
- Have backup prompts ready

**Skills:**
- Comfort with AI concepts
- Basic understanding of web development
- Ability to troubleshoot technical issues

**Time:**
- 90 minutes (can extend to 100-110 if needed)
- Flexible pacing
- Can adjust based on student progress

---

### EDT Edition Requires:

**Preparation:**
- Coordinate with EDT instructor
- Review students' EDT work
- Understand flower vase activity outcomes
- Prepare EDT-specific examples
- Test Bob functionality

**Skills:**
- Comfort with AI concepts
- Understanding of design thinking methodology
- Ability to connect EDT principles to AI
- Facilitation of design discussions

**Time:**
- Strict 90 minutes (less flexible)
- Part 2 (EDT bridge) is time-critical
- May need to make Part 4 optional
- Requires tighter time management

## Recommendations

### Choose Standard Lab 2 If:
- ⭐ This is a standalone workshop
- ⭐ Students have no EDT background
- ⭐ You want maximum flexibility
- ⭐ Focus is on AI capabilities

### Choose EDT Edition If:
- ⭐⭐⭐ Students did EDT activity previous day
- ⭐⭐⭐ You want to show complete design cycle
- ⭐⭐⭐ Coordinating with EDT instructor
- ⭐⭐⭐ Emphasizing design thinking + AI

### Consider Hybrid Approach:
You could also:
- Start with EDT bridge (Part 2 from EDT edition)
- Then follow standard lab structure
- Best of both worlds, but requires 100-110 minutes

## Final Recommendation

**If students completed the EDT flower vase activity the day before, use the EDT Edition.** The continuity and connection between design thinking and AI implementation creates a powerful learning experience that shows the complete cycle from empathy to working prototype.

**If this is a standalone workshop, use the Standard Lab 2.** It provides a complete introduction to agentic AI without requiring prior context.

Both versions are excellent - choose based on your specific situation and learning goals!
